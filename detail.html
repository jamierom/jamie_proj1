Heeseung's New Chapter as EVAN

From leaving ENHYPEN to releasing DEATH OF ME, Heeseung has started a new musical journey, drawing strong reactions from ENGENE.

Leaving ENHYPEN

On March 10, 2026, BELIFT LAB announced that Heeseung would leave ENHYPEN to pursue a different musical direction. He remained signed to BELIFT LAB, while ENHYPEN continued as six members. The announcement surprised many fans because Heeseung had been part of the group since their 2020 debut from the K-pop survival show, I-LAND.

How did Fans React?

Many ENGENE's were dissapointed by the sudden news and hoped that Heeseung could release solo music without leaving the group. Supporters organized petitions and other cammpaigns asking BELIFT LAB to reconsider. Other fans chose to support his new direction while continuing to follow ENHYPEN. There were many opinions on this, but the bottom-line was that Heeseung made a personal choice for his future and fans should respect that.
